# Economy and Rewards

## Resources

### Gold

Gold purchases recruits and may later pay for other army needs. The army has a
treasury, while each persistent soldier also carries a personal gold reserve.
The player starts with enough treasury gold to enter the first recruitment and
combat cycle.

### Espionage points

Some recruits provide espionage points when hired. These points investigate
ranked NPC armies and reveal information before combat that would otherwise
become visible only during battle phases.

## Battle rewards

### Experience

Experience gain increases with a soldier's involvement and risk:

```text
Survived < Fought < Fought, was wounded, and survived
```

Exact values and advancement effects remain open.

### Battlefield loot

Killed soldiers leave their equipment and personal gold. The exact recovery and
ownership rules are not defined yet. Unneeded recovered equipment can be sold.

### Territory

A victorious army exchanges places with the defeated scripted opponent. Places
produce different recurring incomes and may carry properties that affect the
next battle. Conquest can also produce immediate plunder. Exact income timing,
place properties, and loss rules remain open.

## Core economic loop

```text
Starting gold
-> recruit and prepare
-> fight
-> gain experience, loot, territory, and income
-> recruit and prepare at a larger scale
-> challenge a higher-ranked army
```
