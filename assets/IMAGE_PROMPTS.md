# Image prompts

These prompts define the visual family for generated recruitment assets. Keep
the common direction stable when adding variants so individual people and later
group offers feel like parts of the same world.

## Individual recruit portrait

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: small square recruit portrait for a dark antique-war mobile game UI
Primary request: portrait of [AGE AND BACKGROUND] ancient [SOLDIER TYPE] recruit,
[DISTINCTIVE FACE AND EXPRESSION]
Scene/backdrop: plain near-black charcoal backdrop
Style/medium: restrained hand-painted game portrait, slightly graphic, readable
at 64 pixels, not photorealistic
Composition/framing: centered head and shoulders, front three-quarter view, face
fills most of a square canvas
Lighting/mood: warm directional torchlight, grounded and serious
Color palette: muted ochre, bronze, dark olive, charcoal, matching a minimal
black and bronze interface
Constraints: one person only; historically plausible simple clothing; no helmet
covering the face; no text; no border; no watermark; crisp silhouette
```

## Recruitment group

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: square recruitment-group card for a dark antique-war mobile game UI
Primary request: a cohesive recruitment group of [COUNT AND SOLDIER TYPES],
[COMPOSITION OR SOCIAL RELATION]
Scene/backdrop: sparse ancient military camp fading into a near-black charcoal
background
Style/medium: restrained hand-painted game illustration, slightly graphic,
readable as a small card, matching warm realistic recruit portraits
Composition/framing: compact waist-up group, faces clearly separated and
readable, centered square composition
Lighting/mood: warm directional torchlight, grounded and serious
Color palette: muted ochre, bronze, dark olive, charcoal
Constraints: historically plausible ancient clothing and equipment; no modern
objects; no text; no border; no watermark; no banners or symbols
```

Current group variants: infantry, archers, cavalry, and a mixed company.
